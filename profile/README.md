## About

This organisation is home to a broader collection of Paryx's projects, including larger projects, experiments, shared work, older repositories, and projects that simply make more sense living outside of his personal profile.

Over time, projects may be moved here as they grow, change purpose, become less actively maintained, or no longer fit alongside the personal projects on Paryx's main profile. New projects may also be created here from the start when the organisation is a better home for them.

Paryx's personal GitHub profile is primarily focused on personal projects and work that represents what he is currently building, while this organisation provides a wider space for everything beyond that.

Projects here can vary significantly in status. Some are actively developed, some receive occasional updates, some are experimental, and others are preserved for reference or historical purposes. Repositories that are no longer maintained may be explicitly archived, but being part of this organisation does not necessarily mean that a project is inactive.

Individual repositories contain their own documentation and development status where applicable.
